Operations web app for the travel domain · customer mobile app

Trip page

Clickable prototype, six phone screens. Each screen opens on its own and has demo switches beside the phone.

1
Trip page
The whole trip by day, with phase and status switches, option carousels, approve and decline, and insider tips.
2
Option detail
One hotel or flight option opened full screen: photos, why the advisor picked it, pricing and comments.
3
Comments
A comment thread on one option, with advisor replies and an addressed state.
4
Feedback
Rating the planning experience and the trip, with category pills and a free-text note.
5
Day picker
Jumping to a day, or assigning an unscheduled item to one or more days.
6
At a glance
The full trip shape on one timeline, with a status per item and city pills.
