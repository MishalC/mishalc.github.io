Insider Tips

The speedboat from Male to Soneva Jani takes about 40 minutes. Request the sunset transfer for an incredible arrival experience. Pack a change of clothes in your carry-on — luggage sometimes arrives on a later boat.

Book the Crab Shack for at least one dinner — it's on its own island and only seats 10. The chocolate and ice cream rooms are complimentary and open all day. Full board covers everything except premium wines and spirits.

Soneva is a no-shoes resort — you'll be given a bag for your shoes at check-in. Wi-Fi is excellent throughout. The "no news" policy means no TVs, but they're available on request. Bring reef-safe sunscreen.

The observatory has one of the largest telescopes in the southern hemisphere — book a stargazing session on a clear night. The Cinema Paradiso outdoor cinema is magical. Ask about the sandbank picnic — it's unforgettable.
