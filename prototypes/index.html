Prototypes

I built 15 interactive prototypes (AI-assisted) to test product ideas before development. This is the flow they went through. Below are clickable copies of some of them, with names, people and client data replaced. They show what I can do and how I applied it on real projects. I do not own the work.

Problem
Developers found it hard to work out what to build from long PRDs and large documents.
Solution
I turned each idea into a clickable prototype, so developers could see and try the feature before building it. It bridged the gap between the documents and the build.
  • Each line is a step in the flow
  • The moving dot carries one piece of work through the flow. A step lights up when the work reaches it
